Sign in

All rolesDocker: no outside account needed
  • Each person signs in to their own app: dispatcher, loader, driver, store manager.
  • The dispatcher works in the planning office and covers both depots; loaders and drivers are tied to one depot or vehicle; a store manager sees one outlet.
  • The deployed site uses Clerk; the Docker delivery uses this local sign-in (password waypoint-demo).
Proven here
  • A wrong password, a forged session or no session is refused
  • A driver who opens the dispatcher app is sent to the driver app

The dispatcher plans Kandy

DispatcherPlanning office · 1280 px
  • Build the plan: the engine puts 70 of 73 orders on 23 trips with 0 rule problems.
  • VEH057 (refrigerated van) runs the Day 5 example: OUT077 → OUT083 → OUT079 at 99% of its weight.
  • 3 chilled orders cannot go. Try to fit proves it for OUT078: both VEH057 trips are full, 5 trucks cannot reach a van-only outlet, VEH058 is in the workshop, 15 vehicles cannot carry chilled goods.
  • Each deferral already has the reason the store will read. Send plan to depot: loaders and drivers get their trips, the 3 stores are told.
Proven here (every trip, recomputed from the CSVs)
  • One brand and one district per trip; whole orders
  • Weight and volume within each vehicle's limits
  • Chilled only on refrigerated vehicles; van-only outlets only by vans
  • Home depot only; at most two trips per vehicle; workshop vehicles unused
  • Trip minutes = outbound + inter-stop × (orders − 1) + handling
  • Fresh ≤ 270 min from 03:30; Style + Tech ≤ 480 min; delivery windows
  • No deferral was avoidable; "Other" needs a note; a chilled order on a dry truck is refused

DispatcherSystem

  • No vehicle over its weekly fuel quota
